
# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience, identity, or personal characteristics.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and learning from mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting comments, discrimination, and other inappropriate conduct.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of this Code of Conduct and may take appropriate corrective action when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to the project, including issues, pull requests, and discussions.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take actions proportionate to the severity of the behavior, including:
- A warning.
- Temporary suspension from project participation.
- Permanent removal from project participation.
